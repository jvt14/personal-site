# Future additions

These are possible additions to the site. Nothing listed here is on the live pages yet. Each item notes where it would go and what would be needed from Justin.

## Testimonials

**Where it would go:** A section on the Home page, after Selected work and before the closing “Let’s connect” call to action (previously labeled “03 / Perspectives” / “What others say.”).

**What's needed from Justin:** Approved quotes from colleagues or managers, each with the person’s name, title, and explicit permission to publish.

## Contact form

**Where it would go:** The Contact page, alongside the existing LinkedIn invitation.

**What's needed from Justin:** Choose a form service (for example, Formspree), verify the endpoint, and decide how submissions should be routed. The form must not expose a personal email address on the page.

## SEO for a custom domain

**Where it would go:** The `<head>` of every page.

**What's needed from Justin:** The final custom domain, once it is connected. Then the site can add canonical URLs, `og:url` tags, and an absolute `og:image` URL. Those image tags are relative today because no domain has been chosen.

## Side projects

**Where it would go:** A new section, most likely on the Projects page or as a separate page linked from the main navigation.

**What's needed from Justin:** Which side projects to include, and approved descriptions. For now the site focuses on job highlights only, by Justin’s choice.
